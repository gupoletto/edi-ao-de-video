Privacy Policy — Fun Stuff Uploader

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Fun Stuff Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Fun Stuff.

Data accessed

  • youtube.upload: to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read public statistics of the channel's own videos and search public YouTube videos for topic research.

How data is used

The data is used only to publish the channel's videos and to plan future content. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

Storage and revocation

Access credentials are stored privately by the owner. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
